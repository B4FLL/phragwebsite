Privacy & community rules

How old is this data? sightings can be years old. Phragmites may have spread, died back, or been treated since, and some sites were surveyed only once (for example a batch of records from 2009). Treat faded dots as leads to re-check, not current conditions. Not every patch is recorded, and no dot here is a confirmed location of invasive Phragmites unless an expert verified it.
