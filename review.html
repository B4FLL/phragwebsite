    How to review

    Pick a report, check the map and the evidence, then Verify or Reject. The next one opens automatically. Verify means you, as an expert, are confirming the report. If you can't tell, skip it.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • J or ↓ next report
    • K or ↑ previous report
    • V verify · R reject · U return to review
    • X tick or untick for batch actions
    • N write a note · / search · Esc leave a text box

    Working in a spreadsheet

    1. Download spreadsheet saves the reports in the current tab (after your search and filter).
    2. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. In the decision column type verify, reject or undo. Leave it blank to skip. You can fill in expert_note.
    3. Save as CSV, then press Upload decisions. You'll see a summary before anything changes.

    The spreadsheet contains exact locations and reporters' names. Keep it private and delete it when you're done.
