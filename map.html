Small dots are public records from iNaturalist and GBIF (museums, herbaria). Large dots are reports from this site; a solid outline means an expert verified it.

Data age: sightings can be years old. Phragmites may have spread, died back, or been treated since, and some sites were surveyed only once (for example a batch of records from 2009). Treat faded dots as leads to re-check, not current conditions. Not every patch is recorded, and no dot here is a confirmed location of invasive Phragmites unless an expert verified it.
